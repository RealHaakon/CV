# Clench

**Smart mouthguard** — a wearable sensor device for athlete safety.

## About

Ongoing project at the **Eik Lab** at NMBU, developing a smart mouthguard that can measure
**heart rate (HR)**, **blood oxygen saturation (SpO2)**, **movement**, and **head trauma** for athletes.

The project was given to us by [Clench](https://clench.no/), who design and produce custom-fit
mouthguards for athletes. Thanks to Clench for an exciting project.

My work is the **design of the mouthguards and 3D modelling**. Along the way I am learning a lot
about **manufacturing and prototyping**, with both **PCB** and **3D printing** — including our
**resin 3D printer**.
