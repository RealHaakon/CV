# Patronus

**3D modelling and design** for an anti-drone system, in collaboration with
**Kongsberg Defence &amp; Aerospace**.

## About

Ongoing project at the **Eik Lab** at NMBU. Focuses on the mechanical design and 3D modelling
of components for a drone-countermeasure system.
